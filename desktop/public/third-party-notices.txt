LeiBox application adaptations
Source: https://github.com/leishifu666/LeiBox
Reference checkout: f7a4c8acf55bc1cf817d6804373ae224340f3357

The task filter tabs and resize handle adapt the glass-tab-menu spring
integrator and elastic-panel-resize drag handling. Design references
recorded by LeiBox: solutionb and Alvish. No original reference artwork
or videos are included. These adaptations form part of this application.

The status selector also adapts the blister-dropdown pop/arc/landing
trajectory. Its LeiBox-recorded design reference is Adeline / AstraOS.
No reference foil artwork, HTML, video, or audio is included.
